Honeydew

Sweet, warm, and colorful; Honeydew is an animated short film based on the eponymous episode of the TV series The Bear. Set to the notes of Tezeta, we follow the protagonist as he explores the streets and restaurants of the city with the goal of improving his pastry skills.

The short was created in 3D using Blender, and its development included pre-production (research and art direction), production (modeling, shading, rigging, animation), and post-production phases. A website was also created to showcase the development of the project.
