Partito del Pomodoro

What if a tomato got into politics? Proud symbol of Italian gastronomic identity, tomatoes conceal a critical paradox: while celebrated as a cornerstone of national cuisine, they are harvested by exploited workers under the oppressive caporalato system.

Highlighting these contradictions, our speculative scenario envisions Partito del Pomodoro (Tomato Party): its goal is to protect Italian traditions through exponential tomato cultivation, bringing Italy back to its destined glory. Leading this mission is Pomì Pummarolo, the most valiant tomato of all.

As a part of its political campaign the party presents R.O.S.S.A: a device designed to showcase the goals that’ll be achieved by PdP over its mandate, allowing users to become members and get their party card.

Videos for the project were developed referencing formats that have been used by current political parties in Italy to promote (or one could say advertise) themselves. Recalling Berlusconi's notorious "Discesa in campo", the teaser introduces the audience to Partito del Pomodoro, with its leader Pomi portraying it as the only solution in the dramatic landscape of current Italian politics.

To give a thorough understanding of the project, a TV news report about the party was developed. The video provides an in-depth look at a PdP rally offering insights into the party's values, the beliefs and frustrations of its supporters, and its innovative device R.O.S.S.A.
