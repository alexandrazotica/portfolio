What To Fix

Ecology without class struggle is just gardening. What To Fix is an indie magazine born with the intention of pointing out why mainstream ecology is currently failing, highlighting the link between human rights and enviornmentalism.

Alongside the magazine, a board game was developed in order to translate in broader terms the magazine’s ethos and content to a younger audience.

Starting from the content featured in the article “Ecocide is Genocide: What about Palestine?” we developed a series of booklets, postcards, posters and stickers for the foundraiser organized by Art for Palestine in Milan.

The trailer for What To Fix introduces the audience to the project as a whole, starting with its core idea: the unbreakable link between ecology and the fight for human rights. That serves as the foundation to understand both the magazine’s ethos and the purpose of the board game developed.

The video encompasses a combination of motion design, frame by frame animation, rotoscoping, both original and archival footage
