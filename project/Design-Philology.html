Design Philology

Design Philology is an archive on the history of design teaching and research at Politecnico di Milano. The collaboration, conducted in the context of my internship at Politecnico di Milano's Lab Immagine, consisted in the development of two videos, first one being an introduction to the project.

In memory of Alberto Seassaro, one of the founders of Politecnico di Milano’s School of Design, Design Philology commissioned a video tracing and celebrating his story through photographs, sketches, and anecdotes.
