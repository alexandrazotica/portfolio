L'Ultima Cena

L’Ultima Cena is a short film inspired by Martin Scorsese’s directing style. It follows the protagonist, Davide, during an unusual stay at the home of a renowned art critic. What begins as an artistic retreat, however, soon takes an unexpected turn.

Beyond the short film itself, the project encompassed the creation of a teaser, a behind-the-scenes feature, promotional materials, and the visual identity for our fictional production company.
